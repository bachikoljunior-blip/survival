# M.8 Verified reusable recipe memory

## Trigger

A successful implementation, diagnosis, repair, test, migration, optimization, or
recovery method is likely to recur.

## Content

Store a recipe only after supporting verification exists.

Prefer executable scripts and tested utilities over long prose.

A useful recipe contains purpose, applicability, non-applicability, required
inputs and dependencies, exact usage, verification method, limitations, known
failure modes, and last verified project revision.

Before reuse, confirm that assumptions, interfaces, versions, and dependencies
still match.

Record dangerous or repeatedly failed patterns when preserving that knowledge
will prevent recurrence.

Do not turn one-off solutions into permanent abstractions without evidence of
reuse value.

## Stop condition

Deactivate when the recipe is stored and verified, or when the method proves
non-recurring.
